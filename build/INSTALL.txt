NOVUS ORDO — INSTALLING
Designed and constructed by Quincy Gininda

Download the file for your computer:

  WINDOWS
    NovusOrdo-<version>-Windows-Setup.exe     (recommended) run it and follow the steps
    NovusOrdo-<version>-Windows-Portable.exe  runs without installing (starts more slowly)
    If SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC": More info -> Run anyway.

  macOS
    NovusOrdo-<version>-macOS-AppleSilicon.zip   Macs with M1, M2, M3 or M4
    NovusOrdo-<version>-macOS-Intel.zip          older Intel Macs
    Open the zip and drag "Novus Ordo" into Applications. If macOS says the app
    "cannot be opened" or "is damaged", open Terminal and run:
        xattr -cr "/Applications/Novus Ordo.app"
    and on an Apple Silicon Mac also:
        codesign --force --deep --sign - "/Applications/Novus Ordo.app"
    Then right-click the app -> Open (only the first time).

  LINUX (64-bit)
    NovusOrdo-<version>-Linux-x86_64.AppImage   chmod +x it, then run it
    NovusOrdo-<version>-Linux-amd64.deb         sudo apt install ./NovusOrdo-<version>-Linux-amd64.deb

THE FIRST LAUNCH
  1. Enter the licence key from your Gumroad receipt (also in your Gumroad library).
     One licence runs on up to three of your computers.
  2. Read and accept the licence agreement and risk disclosure.
  3. Create your 8-digit ledger key. It unlocks the terminal and protects the
     exchange keys you keep in it. It cannot be recovered — keep it safe.

Start in paper (simulated) mode. Trading carries a high risk of loss; nothing in
Novus Ordo is financial advice. The full operator's manual is included as a PDF.
